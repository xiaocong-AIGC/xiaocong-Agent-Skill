# Archive

Use when a project, sequence, or reusable asset set is complete. A sequence enters archive only after passing `10B_sequence_review.md`.

Process:

1. Record final outputs and their paths.
2. Mark reusable characters (with performance locks), scenes (blockout .blend files are the most reusable asset in the pipeline), props, style anchors, color cards, bible rules, prompts, and reference strategies.
3. Save successful prompts and failed attempts worth remembering, including shots that hit the 3-attempt degrade rule and what finally worked.
4. Consolidate playbook gains: confirm `PLATFORM_PLAYBOOK.md` entries added during the project are dated and complete; promote pilot-verified texture vocabulary to `locked` in `CINEMATOGRAPHY_BIBLE.md`.
5. For series work, lift shared assets (characters, scenes, bible, sound signatures, playbook) to the series-level library so later episodes reference instead of re-locking.

Output:

- Save `11_archive/PROJECT_ARCHIVE.md`.
- Update `11_archive/ASSET_INDEX.md`.

Quality gate:

- Another project (or the next episode) can reuse the archived assets without reading the full chat history.
- No undated playbook entries; no draft vocabulary left marked as locked.
