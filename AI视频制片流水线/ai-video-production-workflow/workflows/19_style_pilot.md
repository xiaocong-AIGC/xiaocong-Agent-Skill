# Style Pilot (风格试拍门)

Use after core assets (style anchor, color card, first character, first scene) exist and before mass shot production. The style anchor and color card may still be `candidate` at this point — passing the pilot is exactly what promotes them to `locked` (see the bootstrap exception in 08A). Cheapest stop-loss point in the pipeline: a failed style costs two test shots here, not a whole episode.

## Process

1. Pick 1–2 representative test shots: one dialogue close/medium shot (tests identity + expression + lip behavior) and one wide or moving shot (tests scene + camera obedience).
2. Generate start frames (08B) and short clips (09) with the normal templates — no shortcuts, the pilot must test the real pipeline.
3. Verify: style consistency between character and scene assets; identity hold across frames; camera-move obedience on the target platform; color card reproduction; safe-area composition.
4. Promote texture vocabulary that worked from draft to locked in `CINEMATOGRAPHY_BIBLE.md`; record platform findings in `PLATFORM_PLAYBOOK.md`.
5. On failure: adjust style anchor / color card / bible wording and re-pilot. Do not enter mass production on a failed pilot unless the user explicitly accepts the risk.

## Output

- Save `10_revision_notes/Pxxx_PILOT_REPORT_vNN.md`: what was tested, verdict per check, vocabulary promoted, playbook entries added.

## Quality gate

- Pilot passes before batch 08B/09 work starts.
- At least one bible texture entry and one playbook entry come out of every pilot, pass or fail.
